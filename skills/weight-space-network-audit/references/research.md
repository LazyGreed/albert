# Research foundations and evidence limits

Source scope matters. These references motivate techniques, not claims that this skill has been validated on all architectures. Consult original sources and current implementation when choosing a method.

- Nusrat & Nusrat, [Weight-space programming for auditing clinical decision networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05341v1). Original small synthetic ReLU audit and direct edits; not a universal correctness certificate. The original uploaded skill's detailed paper-specific workflow is preserved in `relu-audit.md`.
- Shone & Dunlap (Nov 2026), [Returning to the Neuron Basis for Interpretability, Part 1: Answering Three Objections](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/neuron-basis-theory/). Treats SwiGLU MLP neurons ($a = \operatorname{silu}(g\cdot x)(u\cdot x)$, adding $a\cdot w$) as signed, context-gated steering vectors ($+w$/$-w$ under gate-open $g\cdot x>0$) read via J-lens across Llama-3.1-8B, Qwen3-8B/14B, and OLMo-3-32B. Finds true same-sign dual-concept polysemanticity (Type 3) is rare ($\le 1\%$, though real examples exist), output vectors write in an approximately fixed basis across layers, low-J-share neurons (bottom 2%) often align with later attention $W_Q$ queries, and facts rely on parallel one-hop and multi-hop interpretable neuron paths. **Evidence limit:** autoregressive SwiGLU LLMs only; J-lens readouts are `E0` hypotheses that require causal interventions; fact-trace ablations establish necessity, not sufficiency.
- Shone & Dunlap (Nov 2026), [Returning to the Neuron Basis for Interpretability, Part 2: Using Neurons For Safety Applications](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/neuron-basis-applications/). Applies neuron readouts and base-vs-finetune diffs to zero-shot detection/retrieval, steering, blind LoRA auditing (75% vs. 40% from outputs alone across 58 hidden-behavior adapters), per-neuron vote trigger recovery (54% top-50 across 74 backdoored models), and WMDP-cyber unlearning audits (14 of 23 checkpoints still rank the correct answer first under cloze scoring, with top-changed neurons spiking selectively on forget text like a backdoor). **Evidence limit:** neuron-vote trigger recovery degrades sharply under heavy full-MLP finetuning (failed on 24 Qwen2.5 models with 3-16% per-matrix MLP weight shifts); zero-shot probes lag large supervised probes on many tasks and serve as discovery/triage tools, not formal certification.
- Gurnee et al. (July 2026), [Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace](https://transformer-circuits.pub/2026/workspace/) and Ostrow et al. (Oct 2026), [A Mathematical Theory of the Jacobian Lens](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31955). Introduce and analyze the Jacobian lens (J-lens), a first-order refinement of the logit lens mapping intermediate residual-stream directions to vocabulary tokens across layers. **Evidence limit:** strictly scoped to architectures with token unembeddings; do not apply to non-LM vision, SSM, or tabular networks without an equivalent operator map.
- Artiles et al., [The Alien Space of Science](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01092). Idea atoms, coherence and cognitive availability modeled separately. This skill uses a prompt-level heuristic; it does not reproduce the trained scoring models.
- Meng et al., [Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05262). Causal tracing and rank-one factual editing in autoregressive Transformer models. Does not establish all knowledge is localized or other architectures admit the same edits.
- Zhang & Nanda, [Towards Best Practices of Activation Patching in Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042). Intervention metrics and corruption choices affect localization; use controls and sensitivity analysis.
- Yao et al., [Editing Large Language Models: Problems, Methods, and Opportunities](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.13172). Model editing task and benchmark methodology; evaluate specificity, efficacy and generalization rather than edit success alone.
- [Understanding Clipping In Zeroth-Order Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33062), code [SewoongLab/ClipZero](https://github.com/SewoongLab/ClipZero) (`roberta/src/trainer.py:135,251-270,511-600`). With one shared Gaussian direction per tensor per step, an absolute `eps = 1e-3`, and the per-example scalar `delta_i` clamped to `[-c, c]` **before** averaging and **before** multiplying by `z`, clipped zero-order SGD reports higher test accuracy than the unclipped estimator (+1.70 to +2.04 points on SNLI), with `lr * c` constant inside the clipping-dominated regime; clipping implicitly down-weights hard-to-fit examples, which is why it is reported to help under label noise. The paper reports a batch-level clipping comparison alongside the per-example clamp. For us the aggregate clamp is the distinct estimator, because clamping each example's `delta_i * z` to a norm bound after the multiply is a reparameterisation of the per-sample clamp at `c/\|z\|` for a shared direction (measured agreement `8.3e-17`, `references/zeroth-order-optimization.md`), so it is not a separate method. Calibration is reported as a figure only, and its ~0.12 ECE endpoint belongs to the **unclipped** model under label noise, so no clipped-versus-unclipped ECE delta is quotable. **Evidence limit:** prompt-based zero-order fine-tuning of RoBERTa-large (355M) and OPT-1.3B only, with no component-restricted or full-vs-subset zero-order comparison; no cost, memory or wall-clock measurements are supplied, so any cost table is our own measurement; `lr * c` is per-backbone (5e-4 reported on RoBERTa-large; the OPT-1.3B value is **not verified** here and is not quoted) and does not transfer across backbones.
- Malladi et al., [Fine-Tuning Language Models with Just Forward Passes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.17333), code [princeton-nlp/MeZO](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/MeZO). In-place two-forward zero-order SGD for LM fine-tuning; the estimator lineage that the clipping result above modifies, and the reason "forward passes only" is treated as a memory argument. **Evidence limit:** masked and autoregressive LMs up to 66B on prompt-based classification, multiple-choice and generation, with full-parameter and LoRA/prefix variants; prompt-ZO scoped, no vision or state-space architectures, and its own 12x memory / 2x GPU-hour figures are its measurements at its batch and step counts, so no cost number from it is reusable for another backbone; it reports no `lr * c` invariant, and none transfers.
- Zhang et al., [DPZero: Private Fine-Tuning of Language Models without Backpropagation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.09639), code [Liang137/DPZero](https://github.com/Liang137/DPZero). Private zero-order optimization with nearly dimension-independent rates, demonstrated by privately fine-tuning RoBERTa and OPT; the very-large-epsilon limit of this method is where the per-sample clipping effect was first noticed incidentally. **Evidence limit:** privacy-scoped prompt-ZO on RoBERTa and OPT classification tasks only; behaviour at small privacy epsilon says nothing about the non-private clipping result, no cost, memory or wall-clock measurements are supplied for a new backbone, and no `lr * c` constant is reported as transferable.
- Gu & Dao, [Mamba: Linear-Time Sequence Modeling with Selective State Spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.00752). Input-dependent SSM dynamics motivate time/state-aware probes; does not validate the proposed repair experiments.
- [alpha-beta-CROWN](https://github.com/Verified-Intelligence/alpha-beta-CROWN). Neural network verification implementation. Verify operator support and domain assumptions; proof scope is not arbitrary end-to-end certification.

The architecture registry, access tiers, evidence grades, repair ladder, statistical gates, task overlays and alien experiment sketches are engineering extensions. Treat untested cross-architecture interventions as hypotheses; no new empirical claims about large models are made. The original skill's clinical example is a research illustration only.
